# Undergraduate Thesis Summary

**Title:** UAV Ucus Yolunu 2-B ve 3-B Haritalarda Gorsellestirme  
**Author:** Bekir Oruk  
**Department:** Computer Engineering  
**Institution:** Bolu Abant Izzet Baysal University, Faculty of Engineering  
**Year:** 2024  
**Advisor:** Assoc. Prof. Dr. Murat Beken

## Scope

The thesis demonstrates the simulation and visualization of a planned UAV flight between two selected locations. The workflow marks the start and destination on a two-dimensional map, creates synchronized 2D and 3D geographic views, calculates and draws the UAV route, animates the flight, and rotates the camera through a 360-degree panorama at the destination.

The final implementation uses **Taksim Square** and **Camlica Tower** as the demonstration route.

## Core Features

- 2D geographic visualization of start and destination points
- Synchronized 2D and 3D map views
- GPX-based simulated UAV flight track
- WGS84 heading calculation
- EGM96 geoid-height conversion
- Three-dimensional cumulative route distance
- Animated camera following the UAV route
- Live distance, altitude and heading display
- 360-degree destination panorama

## Demonstrated Result

The thesis reports a total tracking distance of approximately **7,532.95 meters** for the demonstrated route.

## Context

The academic motivation discusses UAV use in hazardous mapping and monitoring scenarios, particularly where direct human access may be difficult or risky. The repository focuses on the implemented **simulation and geospatial visualization** component of that thesis.
